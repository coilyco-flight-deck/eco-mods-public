# eco-mods-public

A collection of public C# gameplay mods for [Eco](https://play.eco/), [Strange Loop Games](https://strangeloopgames.com/)' multiplayer survival and simulation game. The mods add new professions, recipes, crafting stations, farming extensions, environmental systems, and mining and quarrying mechanics. Everything targets `Eco.ReferenceAssemblies` (v0.13.0-beta as of Cycle 13) and builds with `dotnet build`; see [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the full project layout, code-generation notes, and conventions.

The mods fall into a few clusters. The **BunWulf** family ([Agricultural](Mods/UserCode/BunWulfAgricultural), [Biochemical](Mods/UserCode/BunWulfBiochemical), [Educational](Mods/UserCode/BunWulfEducational), [HardwareCo](Mods/UserCode/BunWulfHardwareCo)) adds extended farming and crop variety, a plant-based Biochemist profession as an alternative to petrochemical oil, a Librarian profession that can craft skill books across every discipline, and a handful of specialty hardware items. Standalone mods include [DirectCarbonCapture](Mods/UserCode/DirectCarbonCapture) (late-game atmospheric CO2 mitigation), [EcoNil](Mods/UserCode/EcoNil) (weather and moisture mechanics), [MinesQuarries](Mods/UserCode/MinesQuarries) (infinite but expensive mining and quarrying operations, designed around vertical integration), [ShopBoat](Mods/UserCode/ShopBoat) (a mobile shop world object), and [WorldCounter](Mods/UserCode/WorldCounter) (world statistics tracking). Released builds are distributed as `.zip` files at the repo root; AGENTS.md documents the asset packaging and deploy workflow.

This repo is part of a small family of public tooling around [Eco](https://play.eco/). See also [eco-cycle-prep](https://github.com/coilysiren/eco-cycle-prep) for cycle-prep automation (worldgen rolls, Discord announcements, mod sync) and [eco-agent](https://github.com/coilysiren/eco-agent) for the Discord bridge service that runs alongside the game server. The official modkit lives at [StrangeLoopGames/EcoModKit](https://github.com/StrangeLoopGames/EcoModKit), modding docs are at [docs.play.eco](https://docs.play.eco/) and [wiki.play.eco/en/Modding](https://wiki.play.eco/en/Modding), and other community mods are catalogued on [mod.io](https://mod.io/g/eco).
